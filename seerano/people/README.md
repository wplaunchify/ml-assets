# ML Seerano sample people

Ready-made pictures for the sample people that ML Seerano adds on install, so they have faces without an AI step.

- Named `{name-slug}-{1,2,3}.jpg` (e.g. `mei-lin-1.jpg`); picture 1 is the main one.
- 600px wide JPEGs, about 60 KB each.
- AI-generated for ML Seerano (fictional people, not stock photos of real people).
- The plugin reads them from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wplaunchify/ml-assets/main/seerano/people/` (filter `mlsr_sample_pictures_base`).
